## ✨ Major Improvements

### 1. **Enhanced Responsive Design** ⭐⭐⭐
- **5 optimized breakpoints**: 360px, 480px, 640px, 900px, 1920px+
- **Mobile-first approach**: Best experience on phones, scales up to desktop
- **Fluid typography**: Using `clamp()` for automatic scaling
- **Perfect on ALL devices**: iPhone, Android, tablets, desktops

### 2. **Dynamic Animations** ⭐⭐⭐
**9 new smooth animations** added:
- **cardSlideUp**: Cards fade & slide up on entrance (staggered)
- **cardFadeIn**: Smooth opacity transitions
- **cardGlow**: Subtle glow effects on hover
- **buttonShine**: Gradient sweep across buttons
- **buttonPulse**: Pulse ring on interactions
- **float**: Gentle floating motion
- **bounce**: Elastic bounce effects
- **sway**: Subtle side-to-side movement
- **shimmer**: Text shimmer effects

All animations use **GPU acceleration** for smooth **60fps** performance.
